DSP DIAGNOSTIC METHODOLOGY

Motor Current Signature (MCSA)

Detects broken rotor bars, eccentricity, and phase imbalances via modulation sidebands around the carrier without external vibration sensors.

Second-Order Sections (SOS)

Biquad cascade formulation for digital filtering. Eliminates numerical instability and floating-point roundoff errors at high sampling rates (50 kHz).

Hilbert Demodulation

Computes the analytic signal to extract the instantaneous amplitude envelope. Isolates low-frequency torque oscillations and mechanical impacts.

Harmonic & THD Analysis

Tracks triplen and odd harmonics (3rd, 5th, 7th) to assess stator winding asymmetry, negative sequence flux, and core magnetic saturation.
